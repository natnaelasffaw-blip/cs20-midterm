Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions we get, all in one place for your convenience.

Do I need experience to join FORGE?

Not at all! FORGE is designed for all experience levels, whether you're picking up weights for the first time or getting back into a routine. Everyone starts somewhere, and we're here to help you find your footing.

What kind of workouts can I do at FORGE?

Our space is built for strength training, open movement, and building healthy habits. Whether you want to lift weights, work on your mobility, or simply get moving, there's room to find what works for you.

Do I need to follow a specific workout plan?

No. We encourage you to build a routine that fits your goals, schedule, and comfort level. Start small, experiment with different activities, and adjust as you go.

What should I bring for my first visit?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, supportive shoes, and a water bottle. If you're new to the gym, don't worry about having everything figured out before you arrive.

What if I don't know how to use the equipment?

That's okay! Learning is part of the process. Take your time, ask questions, and start with exercises and equipment you're comfortable with. There's no need to know everything on day one.

How can I help keep FORGE welcoming?

Treat others with respect, be responsible with the equipment, and give everyone space to work out comfortably. A little consideration goes a long way toward making the gym a place where everyone feels they belong.

How often should I work out?

There's no single right answer. Start with a schedule that feels manageable and build consistency over time. Even small steps can add up, and rest days are an important part of a balanced routine.

What if I've taken a long break from exercising?

Welcome back! You don't need to pick up where you left off. Start at a comfortable pace, focus on rebuilding your habits, and give yourself time to adjust. Progress isn't about being perfect; it's about continuing forward.
