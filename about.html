Why we exist

A little less pressure.
A lot more possibility.

FORGE is a fictional neighborhood gym built around a simple idea: showing up should feel like a step forward. Our concept brings strength training, open movement space, and a welcoming atmosphere together.

Whether you are learning how to lift or returning after a break, you should have space to build a routine that works for you.

How we share the space

A better gym starts with us.

A few simple habits make a difference for everyone.

  • Return weights and equipment after each use.
  • Wipe down equipment when you finish.
  • Give other members space to train.
  • Ask before sharing equipment with someone.
  • Help keep walkways clear and accessible.
